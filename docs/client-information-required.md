# Information required from the practice owner

No new doctor's details have been assumed. Complete a private copy of `config/client.example.json` under `client-config/`. That directory is ignored by Git. The example deliberately fails validation until placeholders are replaced. Do not put passwords, API keys, database URLs containing passwords, MFA seeds, patient information or encryption keys in this file.

1. Practice identity: approved legal/display name, speciality or tagline, practice number, doctor name, qualifications, primary address and telephone. These populate the singleton `practice_settings` record.
2. Information Officer: name, monitored email address, approved privacy notice, contact handling and retention arrangements. These populate `practice_settings` and the public privacy page.
3. Consent: exact approved full text, version identifier and each summary card's badge, title and body. Acceptance hashes `version::body`. Summary cards are presentation copy; the full text is the signed record. Obtain the owner's approval of both.
4. Facilities: exact name, unique uppercase prefix of two to five letters, active state and display order for every hospital or consulting location. These populate `hospitals`. A facility is not another tenant or legal practice.
5. File numbering: global fallback prefix, format, existing paper convention and the next unused sequence for each facility/year. Supported tokens are `{PREFIX}`, `{YYYY}` and `{SEQ:06}`, each exactly once, with spaces, slashes, dots, underscores or hyphens as separators. The sequence has a minimum width of six digits and does not truncate larger values. Counters are per facility prefix/year. An empty `initial_sequences` means allocation starts at 1. Explicitly approve this; do not guess the starting number or reuse existing paper numbers. Migrating old records/reservations is separate reviewed work.
6. Initial users: full name, individual email and role for each user. Supply at least one administrator and exactly one doctor for this baseline; staff are optional at bootstrap. Additional clinician access requires an explicit practice decision. Admin does not imply doctor access. Every role must enrol MFA.
7. Brand assets: approved PNG header logo, PNG PDF letterhead and a square PNG app icon of at least 512 by 512 pixels. Provide rights to use them and approve rendered PDFs and icon legibility. `logo_path` is the object name in the private `practice-brand` bucket, not a public URL.
8. Deployment identity: short app name, browser title, description, final HTTPS origin, domain ownership/DNS contact, intended region, new Supabase project reference and new Vercel project name/owner. The public identity is applied through deployment variables at build time.
9. Operational decisions: billing owner and monthly export process; handwritten note access and PDF feature approval; device/browser list; recovery contacts; backup/restore owner; support process and incident contacts.
10. Workflow decisions: confirm the existing document categories and whether any additional management reports are actually needed. No extra categories, analytics, appointments, messaging, claims submission or payment functions are introduced here.

The deployment operator generates and stores the new Supabase credentials, unique clinical KEK, cron secret and SMTP credentials separately in the relevant secret stores. The owner supplies information and approvals, not secrets for Git.
